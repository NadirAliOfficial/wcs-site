About Marubusso

A London based investment firm

Marubusso Management

Marubusso Management (U.K) Ltd (the Fund Manager) is a proprietary hedge fund management company. Through its investment programs, it engages in speculative trading of futures, options, swaps and derivatives including contracts offered on commodity exchanges and overseas futures exchanges.

Marubusso Management (U.K) Ltd is incorporated as a United Kingdom limited liability company.

The Fund Manager's books and records are held at its main place of business and will be made available for inspection at the Fund Manager's offices.

Marubusso Capital

Marubusso Capital (U.K) Ltd brings a disciplined approach to financing, trade advisory services, risk management and hedging solutions for transformational growth at scale.

Marubusso Americas

Based in Lima, Peru, Marubusso Americas S.A. invests across Latin America’s real estate, equity and commodity markets, seeking opportunities arising from economic cycles, market dynamics, and structural developments. The business combines regional market expertise with disciplined investment strategies to identify opportunities across key Latin American economies.

Hedge Fund Solutions

Marubusso Fund LLC

Marubusso Fund LLC is a market-neutral specialist hedge fund that uses market-neutral strategies to trade commodities and liquid stocks of some of the largest companies in the world.

01

US Equities and Global Markets

We focus on large cap companies with proven track record and great upside potential in a fast changing world. Our investment is sector driven, with a positive bias for global corporations providing global and local solutions in staple industries with substantial and sustainable growth runway.

Our strategy is to pursue concentrated, long-term ownership of high-quality businesses possessing durable economic moats and trustworthy management. We seek to deploy capital opportunistically during periods of significant market dislocation, purchasing exceptional businesses when temporary pessimism creates attractive valuations.

02

Commodities

Marubusso Fund LLC are specialists in the Oil Markets, especially in Crude Oil and Natural Gas trading. Our software is designed to capture alpha throughout the structure of the oil complex.

03

Artificial Intelligence

Fundamentally a discretionary model, trading is based on our Artificial Intelligence Assisted Active Management System (AIAAMS).

Trading is determined by consistent fundamental analysis of several factors

Geopolitical news Supply side factors Demand side factors Inventory levels Weather OPEC events NON-OPEC events

Typically, we combine fundamental analysis with technical analysis for trend anticipation, profitable trading opportunity identification and deal capture. Marubusso also uses customised proprietary software for specialist statistical and analytical functions which provides trade signals, indicating actionable position entry and exit. Market statistics, trade volume and price action are constantly and consistently analysed by our proprietary software using technical algorithms, indicators and formulas.
